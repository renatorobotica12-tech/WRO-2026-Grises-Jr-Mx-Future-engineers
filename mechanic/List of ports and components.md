## Electronics

* 1 EV3 Brick
* 2 Arduino Nano microcontrollers
* 5 HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors
* 1 HuskyLens camera
* 1 5V voltage converter
* Dupont jumper wires
* LEGO EV3 cables
* 2 LEGO EV3 medium motors

## Hardware

* LEGO EV3 parts
* 3D-printed ultrasonic sensor mounts:

  * 1 mount designed to hold a single sensor
